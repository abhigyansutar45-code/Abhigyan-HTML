Journal & Care

General educational notes on watches. Not manufacturer-specific advice.

Mechanical vs. quartz

Mechanical watches use a wound spring and escapement; quartz watches use a vibrating crystal regulated by a battery. Quartz is typically more accurate, mechanical is prized for craft.

What a tourbillon does

A tourbillon rotates the escapement in a cage to average out the effect of gravity on rate. It is largely a showcase of skill today.

Sapphire crystal

Synthetic sapphire is very scratch resistant, ranking 9 on the Mohs scale, though it can still chip on hard impact.

Care basics

Keep away from strong magnets, rinse after salt water only if rated for it, store mechanical watches dry, and service them periodically.
